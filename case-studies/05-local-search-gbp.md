# 05. Local search across a villa portfolio

[Back to overview](../README.md)

## The situation

Every Spacez villa can have its own Google Business Profile. That's a lot of free visibility on Maps and in local results, and a lot of upkeep. When I started, profiles had mixed naming, some weren't verified, and many pointed to the homepage instead of the villa's own page.

## What I did

**1. Set a listing standard.** One naming pattern, primary category, opening hours, service area, phone and website rule for every villa. Descriptions stay under 750 characters and open with the guest's problem, then the amenities, then a short direct answer to a common question. The standard is in [playbooks/gbp-listing-standard.md](../playbooks/gbp-listing-standard.md).

**2. Pushed verification.** I worked with the operations team on video and in-person verification and on name plates at each villa that match the profile name. By September 2026, 70% of villa profiles were verified, with the rest in the pipeline. Only verified profiles can serve as location assets in Google Ads, so this also helps paid search.

**3. Audited every website link (Nov 2026).** I checked 56 profiles across the business groups. Each one should link to its villa page, so a guest lands on the right villa with photos and dates. I updated 21 profiles to the correct villa URL, using the site's sitemap as the source of truth. Profiles for villas with no live page were left alone and flagged.

**4. Created new profiles as villas joined,** with real photos named and ordered by room.

**5. Covered Bing too.** I keep Bing Places listings in step with Google, since Bing feeds other search and AI tools. By September 2026, 80% of villas were listed there.

**6. Grew reviews the honest way.** Asking real guests for reviews and replying to them on time. No staff reviews on our own profiles.

## What I'd tell the next person

- Before changing a URL anywhere, check what links to it. Google Business Profiles, ads and old posts often point at the old address, and Google will tell every profile owner when a linked page disappears.
- Verification is an operations project as much as a marketing one. Name plates and a person on site matter.
- Check facts on the live villa page every time. A profile that promises a pool the villa doesn't have will cost you a review.
