# 08. Reporting the founders trust

[Back to overview](../README.md)

## The situation

Different tools gave different booking numbers, and a report built from whichever one was open that day wasn't something the founders could plan on.

## What I did

**1. A weekly report every Monday.** It goes to the founders and the team as an email, a WhatsApp-ready image and a one-page PDF. Same structure every week, so changes jump out:

- Google Ads by city (Bengaluru, Mumbai, Hyderabad, total)
- Corporate lead form campaign
- YouTube, App and ChatGPT Ads
- Meta Ads by campaign
- WhatsApp leads and cart abandoners, when available

**2. One source per row.** Spend, clicks and calls come from the ad platforms. Bookings and revenue come from the booking system, filtered to marketing sources, by booking date, cancellations excluded. The rule is written into the report template so anyone can rebuild the numbers.

**3. A quarterly report card.** A short deck for the leadership team: what launched, how each channel did, and what's next. The first one covered June to mid-September 2026.

**4. Next steps live in one place.** The weekly email points to the work plan on the internal dashboard, so the report stays about results.

The empty template is in [playbooks/weekly-report-template.md](../playbooks/weekly-report-template.md).

## What I'd tell the next person

- A report people trust is consistent before it's clever. Keep the format fixed.
- Put the data source next to every number, at least in your own notes.
- Send it on the same day every week. People start planning around it.
