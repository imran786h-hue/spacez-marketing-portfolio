# Blog SEO and schema checklist

[Back to overview](../README.md)

## Brief (fill before writing)

- Headline
- URL slug
- Focus keyphrase, 4 content words or less
- Meta description, 155 characters or less, includes the keyphrase
- Tags, comma separated words
- Alt text for every image

## Writing

- Open with a hook the reader recognises from their own life
- A short direct answer under every question heading
- Mobile-friendly tables, 2 or 3 short columns
- Contractions, light humour, no fake guest quotes
- Every villa fact checked on the live villa page

## Yoast, all checks green

- Keyphrase at the start of the SEO title
- Keyphrase in the intro
- Keyphrase in 30 to 75% of H2 and H3 headings
- Keyphrase density right for the length
- Keyphrase in the alt text of 30 to 75% of images
- About 30% of sentences use transition words
- Real photos with descriptive file names, one set as the featured image

## Schema (JSON-LD, site-wide or per post)

| Type | Where |
|---|---|
| Organization | Site-wide |
| Person | Author pages and posts |
| BreadcrumbList | Every post |
| FAQPage | Posts with a Q&A section, separate from Article |
| VideoObject | Posts with an embedded video |
| Review / AggregateRating | Only pointing to real off-site reviews |

## Site files

- `llms.txt` at the root: brand summary and key pages in plain text
- Sitemap submitted in Search Console and checked for "last read" date
