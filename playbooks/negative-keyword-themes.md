# Negative keyword themes for a villa rental brand

[Back to overview](../README.md)

Themes I keep in shared lists. Add the exact terms you see in your own search terms report rather than copying a long list blind.

| Theme | Example terms | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Buying property | buy, sale, resale, price per sq ft, plots | Real-estate buyers, not guests |
| Long-term renting | lease, monthly rent, rental agreement, PG, hostel, flat for rent | Tenants, not short-stay guests |
| Jobs | jobs, vacancy, hiring, salary, internship | Job seekers |
| Cheap intent | cheap, budget, lowest price | Mismatch with a luxury product |
| Wrong place | names of hill stations or cities you don't serve | Searches for other destinations |
| Look-alike names | other properties or places that share a villa's name | Noise for single-property ad groups |
| Workspace noise (office product) | day pass, hot desk, coworking seat, virtual office | Wrong product for a whole-villa office |
| Events you don't host | wedding hall, banquet, resort day out | Different venue type |

## How I manage them

- Campaign-level shared lists for themes that are always wrong.
- Ad-group negatives for routing, so the right ad group gets the search.
- A short "do not pause" list for terms that look weak early but bring bookings later.
- Weekly review of the search terms report, helped by a script that flags likely junk.
