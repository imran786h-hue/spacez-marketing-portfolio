# Single Property Ad Group (SPAG) template

[Back to overview](../README.md)

A SPAG is one Google Ads ad group for one villa. It catches people who already know the villa's name, or something close to it, and sends them straight to that villa's page.

**When to build one:** the villa gets steady branded or near-branded searches, its landing page converts, and it has something guests remember (a pool, a theatre, a glass house, a location).

**When not to:** the villa page has clicks but no bookings. Fix the page first.

## The pack

| # | Part | Spec |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Headlines | 15, each 30 characters or less. Pin suggestions for H1, H2, H3 |
| 2 | Descriptions | 4, each 90 characters or less. D1 pinned |
| 3 | Positive keywords | 16: 10 exact + 6 phrase |
| 4 | Negatives inside the SPAG | About 25: real-estate intent plus noise specific to this villa's name |
| 5 | Cross-negative | 1 phrase negative of the villa name, added to the other ad groups in the campaign |
| 6 | Sitelinks | 4: Today's Best Offers (to the offers page), 2 villa features, WhatsApp concierge |
| 7 | Callouts | 8 |
| 8 | Display paths | 2, each 15 characters or less |
| 9 | URLs | Final URL and mobile final URL, both the villa page |
| 10 | Starting bid | A conservative max CPC, reviewed after the first two weeks |

## Repeat-guest lines (in every pack)

Many bookings come from guests who've stayed before. Every SPAG speaks to them too:

- H2 pinned: `Welcome Back to [Villa]`
- Unpinned: `Loved It? Book [Villa]`
- D3 points to the offers page
- D4 opens with a "Loved [Brand]?" hook

## Launch checklist

- [ ] Villa page loads fast on mobile and shows real photos
- [ ] Every feature in the copy is on the villa page
- [ ] Cross-negative added to the sibling ad groups
- [ ] Shared negative lists already attached to the campaign
- [ ] Conversion goals on the campaign are booking and chat, nothing else primary
